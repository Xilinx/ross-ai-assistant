# 8-10458: `include directive is not isolated on its own line

**Message:** `[Synth 8-10458] `include directive is not isolated on its own line`
**Severity:** ERROR
**Source:** Oasys Synthesis

---

## Root Cause

A `` `include `` directive shares its line with other code/tokens. Preprocessor directives must appear alone on their own line.

---

## Common Patterns

### P1: `include not isolated on its own line

**Problematic Code:**
```verilog
wire w; `include "defs.vh"
```

**Recommended Fix:**
```verilog
wire w;
`include "defs.vh"
```

---

## Fix Strategy

1. Put the `` `include `` directive on its own line with nothing before or after it.
2. Same rule applies to other compiler directives (`` `define ``, `` `ifdef ``).
3. Check for a missing newline before the directive.
