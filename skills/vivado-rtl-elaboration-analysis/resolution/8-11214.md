# 8-11214: Force Assignment to Resolved Composite Member

**Message:** `[Synth 8-11214] target of simple 'force' assignment cannot be a member of a resolved composite signal`
**Severity:** ERROR
**Source:** Oasys Synthesis

---

## Root Cause

VHDL `force` assignment targets a member of a resolved composite signal, which is not allowed.

---

## Common Patterns

### P1: force on a resolved composite member

**Problematic Code:**
```diff
-- Before: force on resolved composite member
signal s : std_logic_vector(7 downto 0);
s(0) <= force '1';  -- ERROR
```

**Recommended Fix:**
```diff
-- After: force the entire signal or use regular assignment
s <= force x"01";  -- force entire signal
-- Or use regular assignment (force is simulation-only):
s(0) <= '1';
```

---

## Fix Strategy

1. `force`/`release` are simulation-only — remove them from synthesizable RTL and use ordinary signal assignments.
2. Model any intended override with real logic (mux/enable) instead of forcing a resolved composite member.
3. If this is testbench code, keep it out of the synthesized path.
4. Avoid forcing individual members of resolved composite signals even in simulation.
